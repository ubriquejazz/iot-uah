# ESP32-Cam

AI-Thinker Model

The ESP32 Cam has a ESP32 Wifi microcontroller and a 2MP  OV2640 camera. 

Wide application in  various IoT projects, such as smart home appliances, industrial wireless control, wireless monitoring, wireless QR identification, wireless  positioning signals and other IoT applications.
